Ammo = life. You start with 50 balls. Shoot the green ammo cells to refill. Out of balls? Run over.
Getting hit by anything costs 10% of your ammo and drops your gun one level.
Red glow means it's heading straight for you. Shoot those first.
Gun upgrades: shoot a spiky weapon cache to move up a level (Single → Penta).
Combo: hits raise your score multiplier, misses lower it.
Chrono Pulse: fills as you hit. When full, it clears the screen and slows time.
The Monster shows up each level. Aim for its red eye (triple damage) and shoot down its green goo.
