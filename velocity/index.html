More details
1 · The objectiveEvery city has a Series Target: the investor money you need before you can flee. Hit it, reach the airport, move on. Go bankrupt or get arrested and the run ends.
2 · The cityArrow keys / WASD, or tap where you want to walk. It's dark out there: you only see what's near you, but the radar in the corner shows coloured blips for what's around. Walk into someone and the encounter plays out by itself.
3 · Seed is a trialNo rivals, no analysts, no police, no boss. Just retail investors. Learn the controls, take the money, flee.
4 · Hype hardIn every encounter, mash tap or Space. The faster you mash, the stronger your pitch, up to more than double. Push into the striped OVERHYPE zone and you hit even harder, but every tap there adds a little Heat.
5 · Two bossesFrom Series A, every city has two named bosses. The guard waits by the airport. The hunter roams the city looking for CEOs, rivals included, and arrests or bankrupts the ones it catches. Beat either for a big payout and a clean record, or slip past. Lose to the boss and they call the police, and sometimes they have you arrested on the spot. The hotter you already are, the likelier that is.
6 · Runway is healthYour strength is fixed at the city's base, so you can't get lucky. Everyone else rolls the dice. Runway also ticks down like a clock. Losses cut it, wins top it up, and what's left comes with you.
7 · Dogs (all sly)Walk into a glowing stray to hire it. CFO +25% Attack · COO +25% Defend · CHO wildcard. Lose a defence and one may bolt; lose to a rival and they'll take one. Lost roles show up again later.
8 · Fluff & HeatFluff is your AI earpiece's confidence: higher means stronger pitches and a wider view. It drains over time; coffee shops refill it. Heat 100 = arrest. Heat draws trouble to you (the marks on the Heat bar): above 10 analysts come looking for you, above 25 regulators, above 40 the police wake up and chase you until it cools below 30, and above 50 both bosses hunt you down and investors back away. Heat comes with you to the next city, so don't land hot.
LandmarksEvery city has its own landmark on the skyline. Head north to see it. You'll also get a preview of the next one at the airport.
Heat over 50 · market coolingOnce Heat passes 50, investors spot you coming and back away ("NOPE!"). You can still catch them, but you'll have to chase. From Series C, and again every 3 rounds after, the market cools: investors get 15% tougher and Heat builds 25% faster.
9 · StreaksWin encounters back to back for a streak (it resets in each new city). Every win after the first adds +10% cash, up to +50%. One loss and it's gone.
Cash out & disappearAfter clearing any city you can quit while you're ahead. Leave in Seed to Series B and the Guild docks you for cowardice (×0.8). Series C–E pays ×1.2, and Series F onward ×1.5. Leave with the police on you (Heat over 40) and there's no bonus; leave with Runway under 25% and it's a fire sale (×0.9). You go on the board as "Exited — whereabouts unknown."
10 · Airport duty-freeYou skim 20% of every city's raise for yourself. Spend it at the airport on 14 pieces of essential business equipment, from gold sneakers to a fake passport. There's a deal of the day at 40% off, and sometimes a limited-edition item. Everything you own shows under your meters.
11 · RivalsIdentical men in identical suits, wearing sunglasses. They hunt investors, fight each other and come for you. They can go bankrupt, get arrested, or leave and come back later with better bling (★). They get more aggressive every round, hunting you from further away, and every fight with one adds Heat: they report you even when you win.
12 · The city never emptiesEveryone you clear out is replaced over time: investors, analysts, regulators, police, rivals and stray dogs keep walking in from the edges. From Series D, retail investors top out at about $500k each, so you'll need the much bigger institutional cheques to hit the target.
