Welcome, Shaman

Lead your tribe across a hostile world. Build huts, grow your followers, and use spells to break your rivals.

Fog of War

You only see tiles within range of your shaman, followers, fire shooters and huts. Explored ground stays dimly visible; unexplored ground is black. Rival shamans obey the same fog — they can only raid, defend or cast at things their own tribe can see, so a hidden base is safe from raids.

Lands of Interest

Farm — about 50% more followers, +0.15 mana/sec
Forest — +0.8 mana/sec, slows movement
Mine — +1.2 mana/sec, but 20% slower spawning and slow movement
Coast — +0.5 mana/sec, about 18% more followers

Spells & Ranges

All spells are close-range (7–9 tiles) and you can only cast where you can see. Blast (30) wipes out followers in a small area, then needs 5 seconds to recharge. Convert (40) takes nearby enemy followers — but about 1 in 4 can't survive the change and die instead. Tornado (35) leaves a vortex on the ground for a few seconds that grinds down and flings anything inside. Hut (35) builds on flat ground on a Land of Interest.

Followers

➤ Send moves every follower near your shaman to the spot you tap, and they stay there until you send them somewhere else. Badly hurt followers walk back to a hut and heal before rejoining the fight.

Terrain Speed

Plains are fast. Forests, hills and mines slow your followers — plan routes through low ground.

Fire Shooters

About 1 in 5 spawns from any hut is a fire shooter — short ranged (2.4 tiles) but heavier than followers.

Monster

Optional threat. When enabled, 1–4 monsters roam the map attacking everyone — units, shamans, and huts. They seek out huts and hurl violet fire (2.6 tiles range). Each respawns ~40s after dying.

Controls

Tap the world and your shaman walks there — a ripple marks the spot. It strikes anything within melee reach but never chases on its own; to fight something, tap it. Drag to pan. Tap water to snap to the nearest shore. ◎ centres on your shaman.

Spell buttons: tap once to arm a spell for one cast. Double-tap to keep it armed (↻) for repeated casts — tap it again to stop. A red ✕ shows where a cast failed.

Red arrows at the screen edge point to your huts under attack — tap one to jump there. The minimap (top right) shows what you've explored; tap or drag it to look around. ❚❚ pauses.

Audio

Tap 🔊 to mute everything. Tap ⚙ to open Audio Settings and set Music and Sound FX to Off / Low / Med / High independently. Music only begins after your first tap — browsers require a gesture before playing audio.
